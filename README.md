# Career Guide for Business Students

Responsive IS 117 final project for business students preparing for internships and early career opportunities.

## Pages
Home · Business Careers · Resume Tips · Interview Tips · Internships · Important Skills · Contact · Terms of Use · Privacy Statement

## Technology
HTML5, Bootstrap 5.3, and custom CSS in `styles/custom.css`. Original SVG illustrations are stored in `Images/`.

## Contact form
The form demonstrates front-end validation only. It does not transmit or store messages on a server.
