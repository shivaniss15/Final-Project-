PREPARE WITH PURPOSE

Interview Tips

Preparation can help you communicate clearly and show employers how your experiences connect to the role.

Before the interview

  • Research the organization, its products or services, and the position.
  • Review the job description and connect its requirements to your experience.
  • Practice answering common questions out loud.
  • Plan your route or test your video and audio setup.
  • Prepare thoughtful questions for the interviewer.

Use specific examples

For questions about teamwork, challenges, or problem-solving, organize your answer with the STAR approach:

Situation

Briefly explain the context.

Task

Describe your responsibility.

Action

Explain the steps you took.

Result

Share the outcome or what you learned.

After the interview

Send a brief thank-you message within a day. Mention something specific from the conversation, restate your interest, and keep the tone professional.

Build important skills

Practice question

“Tell me about a time you worked with a team to solve a problem.”

Write down one example and practice explaining it in under two minutes.
