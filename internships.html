GAIN EXPERIENCE

Internships

Internships help students explore career interests, practice professional skills, and learn how workplaces operate.

Where to search

Campus career services

Check your college career center, Handshake, career fairs, and campus announcements for opportunities and employer events.

Company websites

Visit the careers section of organizations that interest you. Review role requirements and note application deadlines.

Networking

Ask instructors, classmates, alumni, and people in your community about their career paths. A short informational conversation can help you learn about an industry.

Create an application plan

  1. Choose industries or role types that interest you.
  2. Prepare a resume tailored to each opportunity.
  3. Keep a tracker with company, role, deadline, and application status.
  4. Submit materials before the deadline and save a copy of each application.
  5. Prepare for interviews and follow up professionally.

Application tracker idea

Use a spreadsheet with columns for employer, job title, date applied, deadline, contact, and next step.

Weekly goal

Find two internships that match your interests and review their qualifications. Note one skill you can strengthen before applying.
