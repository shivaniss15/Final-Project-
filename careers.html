Business Careers

Business students can take many paths. Learn what different fields focus on and what kinds of work you may enjoy.

Career areas to explore

Finance

Analyze budgets, investments, and financial performance. Roles include financial analyst, credit analyst, and banking associate.

Marketing

Understand customers and communicate a product’s value through research, branding, campaigns, and digital channels.

Accounting

Prepare financial records, review transactions, support audits, and help organizations follow reporting rules.

Business Analytics

Use data, spreadsheets, and visualization tools to identify patterns and support business decisions.

Management

Coordinate people, projects, and operations so teams can meet organizational goals.

Consulting

Research business challenges, evaluate options, and recommend practical improvements for clients.

How to choose a direction

  1. Notice which classes and projects interest you most.
  2. Read job descriptions and identify tasks you would like to try.
  3. Talk with instructors, alumni, and professionals about their work.
  4. Use a class project, student organization, or internship to test your interests.
Explore internships

Career exploration checklist

  • List three fields that interest you.
  • Find two job titles in each field.
  • Compare required skills.
  • Choose one person to ask for advice.
