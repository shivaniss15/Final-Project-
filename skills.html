Important Skills

Business students benefit from combining technical knowledge with strong communication and collaboration.

Skills employers value

Communication

Share ideas clearly in writing, presentations, meetings, and everyday conversations.

Teamwork

Listen to others, contribute reliably, and work toward shared goals.

Problem-solving

Define a challenge, compare possible solutions, and explain your reasoning.

Time management

Prioritize tasks, plan ahead, and meet deadlines consistently.

Data and technology

Build confidence with spreadsheets, presentations, collaboration platforms, and basic data analysis.

Adaptability

Stay open to feedback, learn new tools, and adjust when priorities change.

Ways to develop your skills

  • Take an active role in group assignments and student organizations.
  • Volunteer for presentations or project coordination.
  • Practice spreadsheet and presentation tools using class projects.
  • Ask for feedback and identify one area to improve each semester.
Send a question

Reflect on your strengths

Write down one example of a time you communicated, solved a problem, or helped a team succeed. That example can support your resume and interview answers.
